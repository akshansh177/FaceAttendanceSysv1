# Product Requirements Document

## Product Name

Face Attendance Management System

## Product Vision

Provide a secure, modern, self-hosted employee attendance platform using face recognition without recurring subscription costs.

---

## Target Users

### Employee

- Check attendance
- View attendance history
- View shifts

### HR

- Manage employees
- Manage attendance
- Generate reports

### Manager

- Monitor team attendance
- View department reports

### Admin

- Full system control
